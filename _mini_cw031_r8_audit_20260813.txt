ITEM 1 -- THE RULING, WITH ITS DECISIVE ARTIFACT.

  Turn 2: other two rated 55% -> inferred all-lines group minted on all four

  Turn 3: the client TAKES THE INVITATION UP: "design consults should stay

  So "spoken and uncorrected" fails even MECHANICALLY: the correction the

  law). An authority that cannot be revoked is not an authority the client
  holds.

THE RULING (mini, under Nick's blessed corollary 2 and section F):
  AN INFERRED GROUP MUST NOT PASS THE GATE. "Spoken and uncorrected" is
  silence-as-agreement, which Nick's own text forbids ("SILENCE MUST NEVER
  READ AS AGREEMENT"). The opt-out the assertion honours must carry the
  client's authority, and only basis=declared carries it.
  THE FIX SHAPE (stronger than flipping the verdict -- the verdict flip
  alone leaves A2/A5 and the stale-group corruption in place):
  1. THE NET STORES NOTHING. Uniform post-write rates at N>=3 -> the receipt
     ASKS ("that's the same rate on all 4 lines -- should I treat them as
     one shared cost structure, or keep them separate?"). A yes is a
     DECLARATION; the router already emits the group for it (round 8 proved
     the declared path live). Kills the A2 clobber, the A5 echo-mint, and
     the false PASS in one move, and removes the inferred basis value
     entirely. The assertion needs no inferred branch: identical rates with
     no declared group = fail (blend wearing per-line clothing) -- honest,
     because the app has ASKED and the client has not yet said. A run that
     ends inside that one-turn window holds on an unanswered material
     question, which is the recovery design's own vocabulary, not a false
     recurrence.
  2. BUILD THE SEPARATION HALF OF THE DOOR. "Keep X separate" must clear
     X's cogs_cost_structure_group(+basis), and any group write must clear
     the old label from rows leaving the group. Today the words land and
     the state stays; that is the words-match-state law broken in the
     artifact.
  3. STATE THE OVERWRITE RULE: an inference never overwrites a declared
     stamp. (With fix 1 the inferred writer disappears; write the rule
     down anyway so it survives the next net someone builds.)
