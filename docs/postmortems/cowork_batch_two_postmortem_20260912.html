Why the run could not be saved in the coherence section

The coherence section is the lender stress test at the end of financials. Deterministic code evaluates the business, walks levers one round at a time, and has exactly three exits: converged (the numbers pass on the stated configuration and the intake completes), parked (the client explicitly defers; the draft stays open), and roadmap (even the most favourable corner fails; no plan ships). So three of the four did converge. None of them could be saved, for four separate reasons:

  1. "Please wrap up the intake" is not an exit. The park detector only recognises the phrases save it for now, park it, pause this / it / here, stop here, hold off (intake_coherence/section.py line 1067). Halloran asked to wrap up at messages 125 and 127 and got a volume lever, then a price lever. Meriwether asked at 131 and 137 and got a price lever, then two new revenue lines. A client who wants to stop with the numbers they have is offered another lever every time, until they happen to say the word "pause".
  2. The park is honest on the backend and dead in the form. Meriwether said "Let's pause this for now" at 139 and the app replied "we'll leave it right here, everything you've told me is saved, whenever you're ready we'll continue exactly where we left off". Status went to parked, the draft stayed open, the state was persisted. Cowork then reports Send and Submit both disabled with no control that restores them (issue filed 03:30:17). Submit is hidden by design while financials are unconfirmed. Why Send dies after a park is not visible from the server; the input is disabled on !planStarted || sending || loading || !detailsCompleteForChat || !draftId and needs a browser reproduction to name the flag.
  3. Converged is not saved either. Sablecreek converged and completed at 02:59:59, then the form submit at 03:25:05 was rejected with 400: primary_growth_lever is required. The submission validator requires a field the ops interview never asked for and never stored. See defect B.
  4. After a failure the channel closes. Castellane's run died at 03:20:42; the draft status became failed and the form disabled Send as well as Submit (Cowork, 03:21:53). Sablecreek's 400 left the same picture. There is no way to correct and resubmit from the client's side of the screen.

On top of that, the walk itself moved the client's own figures. Meriwether's gap grew from 3.97M to 4.57M during the rounds: a price step was applied to the molded fiber line the client had said to leave alone, the client reverted it, and the accepted steps on the other two lines did not cover the rent and opex the client restated at 145,000 a month. Sablecreek's overhead was cut from 4,080,000 to 633,312 a year and its rent from 66,000 to 40,000 a quarter after the client said the lease was signed (messages 92 to 96); Cowork reproduced the rent cut on Halloran at 03:22:10, 23 percent against a stated signed lease.

The two stack failures

blocker A. Castellane: quarter-grid mapping contract fails on a two-dollar rounding difference

03:20:42 · POST_INTAKE:post_intake_mapping_formula_application_invalid@quarter_grid_applied_mapping_formula_application · run 19ff2d93
Violation
lever expenses::Cost of Goods Sold, quarter 6, FINMO 1,316,052 against formula 1,316,054
Why
FINMO builds COGS as the sum of each revenue line's own amount (finmo_model.py line 449). The contract recomputes it as total revenue times the blended ratio, rounds both to whole dollars, and allows a difference of 1 (MAPPING_FORMULA_INT_TOLERANCE, finmo_model.py line 142). On any multi-line business the two arithmetics differ by rounding.
Blast radius
The constant is used at three validation sites, including the balance-sheet driver check. Thistledown had three lines and passed by luck of rounding.
State
Intake data intact, draft status failed, re-runnable once the gate is right.
Note
The pre-existing red test test_source_uses_shared_tolerance_constant, dismissed earlier tonight as not mine, is about this family of tolerance.

blocker B. Sablecreek: submit gate and ops readiness disagree on what "complete" means

03:25:05 · POST /api/financials → 400 invalid_request · errors {"primary_growth_lever": "primary_growth_lever is required"}
Reproduced
In process, with the exact logged payload and the database write blocked, the handler returns the same 400.
Why
Ops readiness is judged on the consultant's proposed finalize object, not on the persisted ops. Every finalize object stored for Sablecreek's ops section carried primary_growth_lever: null, the last one at 02:30:06, a minute before ops closed after the twelve-month-goal question. The persist step stamps an empty string for the missing field. A fallback question for exactly this field exists (intake_consult.py line 10114) and was never asked. The submission validator then requires it.
Others
Castellane, Meriwether, Halloran and Thistledown all hold a lever, captured in ops. Only Sablecreek's interview skipped it.
State
Draft completed, converged, intact. Needs either the ops gate fixed and the question asked, or one field supplied. Not patched tonight.

Defect register, ranked

Cowork filed 15 issues across the batch. Each was checked against the store and the log. All of the ones below are real. Nothing was fixed tonight.

blocker C. Convergence overwrites client-stated rent and opex against an explicit refusal, with no receipt

Sablecreek messages 92 to 96 · reproduced on Halloran per Cowork 03:22:10

Message 92 offered the full cut "only if your space is month-to-month". Message 93: the lease is signed, rent cannot move. Message 94 reported a mature quarter keeping 290,882, a figure only reachable with the full cut applied, and closed with "every number you just set is yours". Message 95 restated rent 22,000 and opex 340,000; message 96 reported the original gap again, which confirms the overwrite had been live and the restatement reverted it. Same say-do family as yesterday's receipt work.

blocker D. An annual figure stored as monthly, with a "just as you specified" receipt

Sablecreek messages 107 to 112

Client: other operating expense should go to 633,312 a year. App: "updated your other operating costs to $633,312 per month just as you specified". Store: 7,599,744 a year, where the client had asked for a cut from 4,080,000. The client had to do the division ("52,776 a month") before it landed. Units family, the mirror of the router's annual-divided-by-twelve.

blocker E. The coherence loop has no "wrap up" exit and the park dead-ends in the form

Halloran 125, 127 · Meriwether 131, 137, 139 · Cowork issue 03:30:17

Detailed above. Four explicit requests to finish with the stated numbers were each answered with another lever. The only recognised exit words are a short fixed list, and the exit they lead to leaves the client with a disabled form.

blocker F. Selecting a convergence option is parsed as a direct-cost edit and the menu comes back

Sablecreek 03:03:05 and 03:04:42 · Cowork issue 03:10:06

The client's choice from the lever menu became a COGS patch from 22% to 22.2% on both lines, the option was never applied, and the same menu was presented again. The client escaped by naming fields explicitly. Cowork later noted the options also render as clickable buttons it did not use.

major G. The reconciliation loop cannot take a cadence correction, so a wrong cadence becomes a wrong price

Halloran messages 98 to 106

Capacity times price times utilization came out at 31.7M against a stated 9.3M. The client named the error twice: private label is an annual programme worth 252,000, charged twelve times a year in the model. The loop accepts price, capacity, utilization or revenue only, answered "we're going in a circle", and the client had to restate the price as 21,000 per period. The line's revenue is right again; the stored unit price is now wrong for anywhere the plan quotes it.

major H. A lever paragraph repeated verbatim four times while the client corrected it

Halloran messages 110, 114, 116, 118, byte-identical

The client corrected private-label volume from 96 to 8 programmes four times, once in the exact "field: value" form the app asks for. Root is defect G: a correction the loop cannot accept changes nothing in the store, and an unchanged store re-renders the same lever text. Whether the response lock replayed the identical prompt is not visible, because the server does not log store hits.

major I. A price step applied to a line the client said to leave alone

Meriwether messages 133 to 136

"The moulded fibre is the one the distributors beat us down on, so leave that." The pricing round moved it from 0.28 to 0.34 anyway. The client reverted it, and the loop offered the same pricing round again with the moved figure as the starting point.

major J. "Nine hundred thousand" parsed as 900 and surfaced as an orphan figure

Sablecreek messages 93 to 95

The client wrote "ten thousand of a nine hundred thousand gap", quoting the app's own 904,510. The figure detector extracted 900 and asked which line it belongs to. Two parts: the spoken-magnitude parser drops the multiplier, and the orphan-figure guard does not exclude a number that echoes the app's own last message. Nothing was recorded, so no plan impact.

major K. Roster rows landed through the consultant handover skip the payroll rollup

Thistledown turn 71 · Sablecreek 02:37 · consultant handover on the second named person on all five drafts

The router misreads "Yes, Name, title, years, wage" every time and hands it to the people consultant, which writes the row correctly and never runs the rollup. The payroll basis lagged the roster by the second person's wage until the next financials sync. Store-proven on Sablecreek: Kwabena on the roster at 165,000, basis holding only Perrine at 190,000. Healed at financials on every run; plan impact zero tonight; a receipt window where any spoken total is short by one salary.

note L. Suggested year-one hires "needed from launch" on established businesses

Sablecreek: four roles, 406,332 in year one, on a business at 5.3M revenue

By design: the roles were put to the client with wages and timing at message 54, accepted with "no changes", and the rest-of-team question then excluded listed people. The consent is implicit in a narrative review, and a "from launch" field-operations manager on an operating business reads as a launch-stage reflex. Thistledown, a 28-year shop, was offered none.

major L2. Convergence recommends the option that closes one percent of the gap

Sablecreek message 92 · Cowork issue filed at stop

With a 904,510 gap, the app offered option 1 (closes 904,510), option 2 (trim direct costs, closes 9,893) and option 3, and said of option 2: "This is the one I'd suggest." The recommendation picked the smallest closure. The client noticed ("option two only closes about ten thousand of a nine hundred thousand gap"), which is the sentence defect J then misparsed.

note L3. Cowork reads the suggested roles as fabricated payroll; the store agrees on the arithmetic

Sablecreek · two Cowork issues filed at stop: "fabricates four nameless staffed roles with invented wages" and "phantom payroll flows into the shortfall verdict"

Cowork's numbers are right: stated 190,000 + 165,000 + 1,780,000 = 2,135,000, stored payroll year one 2,542,142.50, the difference being the four OEWS-priced suggested roles. Where we differ is consent: the roles were shown with wages and timing at message 54 and accepted with "no changes". Whether a "no changes" to a narrative review counts as agreeing to 406,000 of new payroll, and whether that payroll should feed the lender verdict a minute later, is a ruling, not a bug read. Recorded as L above.

note M. Tooling: the persona watcher loses the thread under parallel drafts

Its vitals for Meriwether reported one turn while the server log showed thirty. It follows one draft at a time and its counters are wrong when four run at once. The four-draft watch armed from the server log was reliable.

What the first run proved

Thistledown, run alone, is the clean end-to-end run Step 2 was waiting on. Intake with 125 messages and no hold; post-intake in 370 seconds, converged, 236 check rows with 0 FAIL and Checks!B2 = OK with cached values; a written plan of 10 figures and 8 tables. Missoula was at 23:40 on the 11th when the plan was built, and the plan carries intake 2026-09-11, prepared 2026-09-11, projections from 2026-10-01. Under the old clock it would have said the 12th. One observation: the intake handler took the tester's Eastern browser date while the plan used Montana's, because the browser's date is not persisted on the draft; a real client's browser and state agree.
