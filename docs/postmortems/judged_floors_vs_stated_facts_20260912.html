Where a judged floor can overrule a stated fact

Every place a GPT-authored floor, ceiling, band or class limit meets a figure the client stated, and what the code does when they disagree. Read against the ruling below. Twenty-six sites read; nine flagged.

"A client's stated cost is a fact. A judged industry band is an estimate. When they disagree, the fact wins. A judged floor may inform a class or a sanity check. It may not replace a stated fact, and it may not be the thing that decides a business is unfundable."
26sites where judged meets stated
9flagged: judged replaces a fact or decides the verdict
3areas: intake walk, restructure solver, payroll

The nine flags, most consequential first

  1. The restructure solver raises stated costs to judged floors and ships them. joint_solver.py:219-243 sets the payroll, rent and cost-ratio boxes with the judged minimum as the lower bound and no comparison to the stated level; a stated materials share of 6% against a judged floor of 14% becomes the box (0.14, 0.14). Lines 606-627 then clamp the as-stated base up into that box, by the comment's own admission, "so the candidate we verify is the candidate we ship." This is the fact replaced.
  2. The searcher can only land on the judged floor when the client is leaner. searcher.py:655-692 collapses every payroll, rent and cost-ratio search level onto the judged minimum when the stated level sits below it; 975-1036 clamps the shipped directive to the same floors and records it as an invariant clamp. Visible, but the judged value is what ships.
  3. A stated-lean payroll share can hard-fail a run. The class-minimum check (schedule.py:4714-4768) is demoted to advisory at schedule.py:82, but fail_fast.py:741-770 re-raises the same violation inside the stage-ramp assertion, bypassing the demotion.
  4. The payroll contract rejects a lean business symmetrically with an expensive one. set_payroll_schedule.py:203-213 and 537-578: a target below the judged class minimum is refused with no stated-fact override, exactly as a target above the maximum is.
  5. A plan that passes on stated facts can be blocked by a judged payroll class. section.py:2753-2801: with every profit test passed, the payroll-share wall (judged labour class times a mirrored policy maximum) pops the converged status and refuses to close the plan.
  6. The corner is a judged construct that decides before the walk. controller.py:1356-1407 with evaluator.py:549-560: judged ceilings, judged floors and judged new lines at cap, tested against judged bands. New lines at 30 to 38% margin broke the 88% gross-margin floor on Brightwater; the retry without new lines (12 Sep) is the only guard.
  7. The roadmap narrates a GPT payroll floor as a fact about the business. controller.py:1433-1438: "the least a team like yours can realistically cost is $X/yr", with no comparison to what the client said they pay.
  8. The part-time branch replaces a stated wage before the stated-wage guard runs. schedule.py:1812-1874 rescales the FTE and writes the OEWS floor into the wage for any title reading "part-time"; the client_override guard at 1875 never sees it. The dollars survive, the stated wage does not.
  9. Judged thresholds are the only pass or fail at the gate. evaluator.py:85-88 and 190-211: five inequalities against judged (or fallback-constant) values decide whether a business enters bounds, corner and roadmap at all. Stated-better simply passes; stated-worse starts the chain.

Area 1: the intake walk (intake_coherence)

Mostly the good pattern: min(stated, floor). A judged floor above the stated figure is discarded; a floor below it is the optimistic corner or the cut's limit.

evaluator.py:564-576 · corner payroll, rent, COGS, G&A, marketing

min(base.payroll_quarterly, payroll_floor_q), min(base.rent_quarterly, rent_floor_q), _floor_pct: min(current, fv). A judged floor never raises a stated cost in the corner. fact wins

controller.py:601-704 · cost cut moves

A cut move exists only when the judged floor is below the stated level; the essentials judge can only make a cut shallower (max(cogs_floor, essentials floor)). A floor above the stated figure means no move, never a rewrite. fact wins

controller.py:714-742 · payroll move

Exists only when the judged team floor is below stated payroll; staffed-dominant teams get no cut move at all. fact wins

controller.py:180-205 · price and volume ceilings

max(1.0, min(pmax, fact / cur)): the judged ceiling is pinned in dollars against the authoring-time stated price; a client move cannot ratchet it. Caps an offer, never a stated price. fact wins

controller.py:1356-1407 · corner_check

The corner is judged ceilings times judged volumes with judged new lines at cap, tested against judged bands; any failure routes to roadmap before the walk starts. Retries without new lines since 12 Sep. decides unfundable

controller.py:1433-1438 · roadmap payroll milestone

The judged team floor is read to the client as what their team "can realistically cost". narrates a judgment as fact

walls.py:112-123 · payroll share class bounds

"passed": share <= max_pct; the class minimum is reported only. A leaner-than-class business does not fail the wall. label only on the low side

section.py:2753-2801 · payroll wall hold

Profit tests passed on stated facts; the judged class times the mirrored maximum still refuses to close the plan ("I can't close the plan on these numbers"). Exits are named in dollars, but the band decides. blocks completion

section.py:372-592 · margin band authoring

The judgment is validated against the measured stated cost placement; a contradiction re-authors once, then holds for a human. "Judgment stays the owner, nothing is clamped." facts arbitrate the judgment

section.py:428-452 · identity change clears a roadmap

A corrected stated fact re-keys the identity and pops the roadmap verdict. fact wins

section.py:818-935 · bounds authoring

Author failure raises a hold, never "no believable region"; the market price ceiling is frozen per market slice and only a stated market fact reopens it. fact wins

section.py:938-965 · Q20 hold

Judged Q20 low, stamped for the panel only. label only

section.py:1630 · custom price clamp

A requested price is clamped between the stated current price and the judged ceiling, flagged as clamped. A request, not a recorded fact. clamps a wish

section.py:2487-2565 · anchor vs ops hold

Stated revenue against stated capacity times stated price: both sides facts; a disagreement becomes a question. asks

evaluator.py:85-88, 190-211 · thresholds and the five tests

Judged gross-margin floor, burden maximum, band low and net-margin floor (constants only when no judgment exists) are the sole pass or fail that starts the bounds, corner and roadmap chain. decides the chain

Area 2: the restructure solver (post_intake_restructure)

The pattern flips here: floors are one-directional and the stated base is clamped up into the judged box.

constraint_author.py:542-544 · owner wage raises the team floor

if team_min < owner_wage: team_min = owner_wage. A stated wage overrides a lower judged floor. fact wins

constraint_author.py:551-559 · cost floors railed against constants only

Judged COGS, marketing and G&A minimums are never compared with the stated percents at authoring; a floor above a stated cost enters the system intact. entry point

joint_solver.py:219-243 · lever boxes

team_lo = min_annual_payroll * bf / 4 with no min() against the stated level; rent_lo = min_quarterly_rent; cost ratios (floor, max(floor, base)). Stated 6% against judged 14% becomes (0.14, 0.14). replaces the fact

joint_solver.py:606-627 · _inv_clamp

min(max(value, lo), max(lo, hi)) on payroll, rent and all three cost ratios at Q11; the comment names the cause (Redux: stated payroll below the judged floor) and chooses to clamp the stated base up. replaces the fact

searcher.py:655-692, 975-1036 · search levels and the shipped directive

When the judged floor is above the stated cost, the only searchable level is the floor and the stated value is not among the levels; the directive is clamped to the same floors and the clamp recorded. replaces the fact

joint_solver.py:334-371 · judged EBITDA and NI floors

Set how hard the rescue must work; no stated figure replaced. sets the target

client_bounds.py:190, 209 · client caps

min(judged, fact): client-side facts tighten judged caps downward. fact wins
